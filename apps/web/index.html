How it works

From invite code to first transition in a few minutes.

Obsidian is not another DJ app. It sits between your mixer and the internet and stays out of the way while you play.

  1. 01

    Open a booth

    Create a private room and send the invite code to the DJ you want to play with.

    INVITE · K7QX-M2PD
  2. 02

    Plug in your mixer

    Pick the audio input your mixer feeds. Your DJ software and hardware stay exactly as they are.

    INPUT · MIXER REC OUT
  3. 03

    Run the booth check

    Obsidian tests both connections and both signals, then tells you in plain words if you are good to go.

    CONNECTION · EXCELLENT
  4. 04

    Go back-to-back

    One DJ is on air, the other cues in their headphones. When it's your turn, take over and the mix moves to your side.

    TAKE OVER

Internet delay is real. We measure it and build the handoff around it.

  • One DJ owns the mix at a time.The DJ on air plays to the room. The other DJ hears them and lines up the next track, like sharing a booth.
  • Handoffs land on the beat.The take over is timed to the delay between you, so the crowd hears one clean transition.
  • Diagnostics when you want them.Most of the time you see Connection, Booth Sync and Remote DJ. The raw numbers are one click away.
