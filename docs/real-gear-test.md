# First test with real DJ gear (rekordbox on Windows)

For the DJ who brings his own controller or mixer. Brian runs the room as
usual, on the built-in deck or on his own gear. Takes about 15 minutes.

## Before you start

- The newest Obsidian on your Windows laptop (same download link as before;
  close the app, run the installer again).
- rekordbox open with a few tracks loaded, your controller or mixer plugged
  in as you normally play.
- A way to hear Brian: earbuds or a second pair of headphones in the
  laptop's headphone jack. Keep your usual headphones on your gear for
  cueing.

## 1. Pick what you send

1. In Obsidian, type Brian's code under **Join a booth** and click
   **JOIN ROOM**. You land on Booth Check.
2. Under **What you send**, pick **Everything this laptop plays**.
3. Play a track in rekordbox and watch the **YOUR SEND** meter on that
   screen.
   - **Meter moves:** good, go to step 2.
   - **Meter stays flat:** rekordbox is sending straight to your gear
     (its ASIO driver), and Windows can't hear that. Open **What you send**
     again and look for your controller or mixer by name (for example
     "DDJ…" or "DJM…"). Pick it and play again.
   - **Still flat:** run a cable from your gear's **BOOTH** or **REC** out
     into a USB audio interface and pick that interface. Or, for this test
     only, set rekordbox's audio output to the laptop (rekordbox
     Preferences → Audio) and pick **Everything this laptop plays**.

## 2. Pick where you listen

Under **Where you listen**, pick the laptop's headphone jack (the earbuds).
That's where Brian's mix comes in. Don't pick your controller here: rekordbox
is using it.

## 3. Booth Check and play

1. Press **RUN BOOTH CHECK**, then **I'M READY** when it passes.
2. Keep Obsidian's **YOUR FADER** at 100% and mix with your own faders
   and EQs, exactly as you'd play. Obsidian sends whatever comes out of
   your gear.

## 4. Trading the booth

- **Your turn:** cue your track, press **R** (ready), then **Space** to ask
  for the booth. Brian lets you in, or you go on air after 10 seconds.
- **Brian asks:** you'll see "BRIAN ASKS FOR THE BOOTH". Press **Space** to
  let him in, or **N** for "not yet" if you're on a roll.
- Blend on your own faders: bring yours down as his comes in.

## 5. Tell Brian

- Which gear and which "What you send" option worked.
- How your mix sounded on his side, and his on yours (any clicks, gaps,
  lag).
- How asking and passing the booth felt.
